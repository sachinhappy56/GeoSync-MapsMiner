# Privacy Policy & Data Compliance Guidelines

**Last Updated:** September 2026  
**Applies to:** GeoSync MapsMiner™ (All Editions)

---

## 1. Our Privacy Philosophy: Absolute Local Data Isolation

At **GeoSync Technologies**, we believe that tools designed for data professionals should respect your privacy above all else. Unlike cloud-based SaaS scrapers that store your customer lists, search perimeters, and extracted leads on their centralized servers, **GeoSync MapsMiner™ operates on a 100% local, zero-telemetry architecture**.

### What We DO NOT Collect:
- :x: **No Telemetry**: We do not collect usage metrics, clicks, session lengths, or feature usage statistics.
- :x: **No Search Queries**: We never see what postal codes, cities, or categories you mine.
- :x: **No Harvested Data**: We do not receive, store, or have access to any business records, contact details, or CSV exports generated by your machine.
- :x: **No Cloud Syncing**: Your database (`collector_data.db`) lives solely on your physical storage drive.

---

## 2. Nature of Harvested Data

GeoSync MapsMiner is a geographic intelligence tool designed to index **publicly accessible commercial directory listings** from mapping services and open spatial registries (such as Google Maps and OpenStreetMap).

The records extracted by the software typically include:
- Registered business titles and trade names.
- Public commercial street addresses and geographic coordinates.
- Public customer-facing telephone numbers and websites.
- Publicly posted customer review ratings and operational hours.

> [!NOTE]
> MapsMiner is explicitly engineered to index **public commercial establishments**. It is not designed or intended to collect private personal residences, unlisted private telephone numbers, or non-commercial private personal data.

## 3. Regulatory Compliance & Best Practices for Users

As an operator utilizing GeoSync MapsMiner to harvest public commercial records, you are responsible for ensuring that your subsequent use of this data complies with relevant regional laws and regulations. We strongly advise adhering to the following compliance frameworks:

### General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK-GDPR)
- **B2B vs. B2C Distinction**: In European jurisdictions, public information regarding corporate entities (limited companies, corporations) is treated differently than personal consumer data. However, data pertaining to sole traders, individual contractors, or partnerships may still fall within the scope of personal data.
- **Lawful Basis for Processing**: When storing or contacting individuals identified in B2B data, ensure you have a documented lawful basis (such as *Legitimate Interest* under Article 6(1)(f) of GDPR).
- **Right to Erasure (Opt-Out)**: Always maintain a suppression list and honor opt-out or deletion requests promptly.

### CAN-SPAM Act (United States) & CASL (Canada)
If you utilize harvested contact details for commercial email outreach:
- Clearly identify the message as an advertisement.
- Include a valid physical postal address for your business.
- Provide a clear, conspicuous, and automated opt-out / unsubscribe mechanism.
- Never use misleading headers, deceptive sender names, or deceptive subject lines.

### Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA - United States)
- Do not utilize automated telephone dialing systems (autodialers) or artificial/prerecorded voice messages to contact wireless numbers without appropriate consent.
- Cross-reference harvested telephone records against the National Do Not Call (DNC) Registry before launching voice telemarketing campaigns.

## 4. User Data Minimization Recommendations

To maintain optimal data hygiene and minimize compliance exposure:
1. **Targeted Extraction**: Mine only the categories and postal sectors strictly necessary for your business analysis.
2. **Scheduled Pruning**: Periodically archive or delete historical export files and clear stale records from your local database once your market research or sales campaign is complete.
3. **Data Security on Host Machine**: Protect your local workstation with full-disk encryption (such as Windows BitLocker) to ensure that harvested database files are secured against physical theft.
